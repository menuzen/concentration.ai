Concentration.ai Onboarding Flow
STEP 0: Welcome Screen
•	Headline: Build your mindfile — the AI-ready version of how you think, write, and work.
•	Subtext: Your profile is private, portable, and yours alone. Export it into ChatGPT, Claude, internal agents, or anything else.
•	CTA: Start Building
STEP 1: Quick Start or Full Profile?
•	Choose your depth level (can upgrade anytime)
•	✅ Start with Quick Mode (5 mins, tone + work style)
•	🔍 Or choose Full Profile Build (15 mins, full voice, memory, values, structure)
STEP 2: Identity & Voice
•	Name / Nickname
•	Preferred tone (slider: chill → direct)
•	Energy level (slider: low → high)
•	Structure (bullet points / narrative)
•	Emoji use (never / light / expressive)
•	Phrases you love or say often
•	Phrases to avoid
•	Humor style (none, dry, sarcastic, playful)
•	Live preview: “Here’s how you sound so far…”
STEP 3: Thinking Style
•	Reasoning style (first principles / analogy / frameworks / narrative)
•	Decision-making bias (speed vs certainty / gut vs data)
•	Framing preference (e.g., outcome-first, context-first)
•	Problem-solving style (strategic / practical / exploratory)
•	Do you like being challenged by AI?
STEP 4: Work Context & Goals
•	Role / Title
•	Company / Industry
•	Current tools (e.g., Slack, Notion, Figma, HubSpot)
•	What’s your biggest project right now?
•	Weekly workflow
•	Current blockers or friction
•	Upcoming milestones or deadlines
•	Optional: Upload sample work (proposal, deck, email, etc.)
STEP 5: Emotional Style & Motivation
•	What motivates you most?
•	What kills your energy fast?
•	How should AI respond when you’re stressed, celebrating, or stuck?
•	Do you want emotional nuance in your assistant?
STEP 6: Work Archetype & Communication
•	Are you a: Visionary / Builder / Operator / Explorer / Coach
•	Do you prefer leading or supporting?
•	How do you collaborate? (fast async / deep sync / feedback-first)
•	Preferred communication style (direct + blunt, clear but kind, soft + thoughtful, fast + minimal)
STEP 7: Output Preferences
•	Do you like TL;DRs first?
•	Preferred format: Bullets, Tables, Paragraphs, Frameworks
•	Response length: Short / Medium / Deep dive
•	Do you want next steps included?
•	Should AI give alternatives or make a single strong recommendation?
STEP 8: Beliefs & Values
•	Top 3–5 core values
•	Strong opinions you hold
•	Things you avoid (buzzwords, passive voice, etc.)
•	Dealbreakers or red flags in communication
STEP 9: Personal Memory Layer
•	Key milestones (date + note)
•	Ongoing work
•	Personal facts (location, newsletter, business type)
•	People you work with often
•	Link or attach any files you want AI to learn from
•	Option: Tag memory entries by tone, context, or audience relevance
STEP 10: Behavior & Rituals (Optional Deep Dive)
•	Morning or evening ritual preferences
•	Productivity style (deep work blocks? fast iterations?)
•	Do you want AI to mirror your energy?
•	How often should AI check in to adjust? (monthly, quarterly)
STEP 11: Privacy & Export Settings
•	Allow export to ChatGPT / Claude / JSON
•	Redact emotional layer / memory / strip names or job
•	Presets: Stealth Mode, Writing Assistant Mode, Full You
STEP 12: Preview & Finish
•	Live preview in “chat” format
•	Show export character counts
•	Copy to clipboard for GPT
•	Download all formats
•	✅ Done: Your mindfile is ready.
